Common questions

How do I pick a restaurant?

On Tonight, choose a city and meal, then use Pick for me or draw a card with a tap or swipe. Open its details to save a favorite or get directions. Use Pick another for a different suggestion.

Do I need internet or an account?

The bundled restaurant catalog supports offline picking without sign-in or a subscription. Photos, Maps, external links and iCloud sync may need internet. Location access is optional; you can select a city manually.

Where are my saved trips and diary entries?

Open Account → Saved library & recovery to access existing favorites, collections, trips, diary entries and documents. Use JSON export to keep a copy of your saved records.

Why are favorites missing on another device?

Favorites and city preferences can sync through private iCloud when available. Check that both devices use the same Apple Account, iCloud is enabled and each device has internet access. Keep an export of your saved records before reinstalling the app.

A restaurant’s information is out of date. What should I do?

The catalog is bundled with the app, so restaurant information can change between updates. Check opening hours and availability with the venue before visiting. Contact support with the city and restaurant name to report a correction.

How do I manage an earlier subscription?

No subscription is required for current restaurant picking. To manage an existing Apple subscription, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions. For purchase or refund help, visit Apple’s purchase support.
